# SIS-Main

**System Intelligence Science (SIS)** research registry and scientific-program workspace.

## Literature

- [SIS v0.3.1 Literature Re-Coding Registry](literature/SIS_v0.3.1_Literature_Re-Coding_Registry.md)

The registry re-codes literature previously reviewed in the ARSO research stream under the SIS v0.3.1 scientific semantics: Primary Program, Framework Capability Profile, primary estimand, claim maturity, identification strength, boundary/transport evidence, and evidence role.

## Governing principle

Implementation forms such as agents, multi-agent systems, RAG, workflows, tools, memory, harnesses, and prompt optimizers are **not** treated as first-principles scientific primitives. Papers are coded by the scientific question and estimand they actually support.
