## Scientific objection to accepting SCF/SDR/ASR as a reliable taxonomy

**Claim:** The existing three Primary Programs are *conceptually separable by the PrimaryEstimand an author supplies*, but independent scientific discriminant validity and **exhaustive coverage** are not established by 50 authored claim fixtures, 48 authored blinded question stimuli, 12 bounded toy countermodels or 58/58 source/fixture consistency checks.

### A. Author-written Estimand can cause a tautological classification

A feedback-driven Agent may choose to add specialists, change system graph dynamics and improve long-horizon recovery. The same experiment may be described as ASR (conditional choice), SDR (time-dependent trajectory), or SCF (system property effect of an organization mechanism). If coders can obtain different classifications under semantically equivalent scientific-contract descriptions or because the single PrimaryEstimand was not recoverable, §43's precedence cannot rescue it. R5.1 §44 permits splitting into **different claims** only if their different targets and evidence assumptions remain explicit.

**Required falsifier of the objection:** two independent annotators, hidden from candidate labels, identify the same target and program on genuinely equivalent/expert-adjudicated inputs, including deliberately ambiguous abstracts; disagreements are analyzed rather than overwritten by author's preferred labels. R5.1 §166 `RA2=UNTESTED` because no independent A/B response or adjudicated Gold exists. The SAR-03-B exploratory κ threshold is **not** an official SIS norm.

### B. An irreducible joint feedback/structure/trajectory estimand could exist

Define a proposed scientific question about policy-driven component birth/retirement, dynamically changed transition laws, and long-horizon risk/cost-constrained system viability. A claimed joint estimand may be described using policy, trajectory or structure. **This alone does not prove a fourth Primary Program:** it may be losslessly expressed in ASR, SDR or SCF based on the actual primary causal target. But **if independently established `Q*` cannot be losslessly expressed** under SCF/SDR/ASR (including CSCI), R5.1 §165 `RA1` permits reopening.

**Test:** pre-register full joint estimand and admissible intervention, then attempt each lossless three-program reduction without using labels or choosing a different target. Mark `ROUTING_UNRESOLVED` where unidentifiable, rather than inventing a Program.

### C. M1/M2 can change the causal unit even when observed outputs match

A new procedure P can be part of interpreter I's state (known `C*` M1) or a separately permissioned component (proposed dynamic `C_t` M2). SAR-03-B's bounded `Y_A=I·P` versus `Y_B=I` shows identical baseline output and divergent `do(P=0)` responses. Thus observational or performance equivalence alone does not establish identity, intervention or causal-estimand equivalence. Stable IDs, creator, generation, version, effective graph, authority, legal information and intervention correspondence may be necessary. If mapping is missing, keep a review-only comparability warning; if the actual common primary estimand is unknown, keep canonical `ROUTING_UNRESOLVED`.
