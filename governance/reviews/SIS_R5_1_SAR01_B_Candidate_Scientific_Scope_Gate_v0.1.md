# SAR-01-B Scientific Scope Gate (Review Candidate v0.1)

> **PROPOSED / REVIEW ONLY.** Not an SIS R5.1 Core primitive, enum, schema change or formal release. The master specification remains unchanged.

## Five checks

- **S1 — Scientific system referent.** Specify the focal system and optional focal component, its task distribution, boundary, environment, information, resource and risk conditions. Do not use LLM presence, deep learning, agent branding or number of components as a prerequisite.
- **S2 — Conditional target property.** Declare the system-level target: capability, efficiency, viability, robustness, adaptability, capability frontier or a carefully operationalized higher-order system-intelligence construct. A standalone model classification score or plain algorithm runtime does not by itself establish a system-level SIS scientific claim.
- **S3 — Scientific question and estimand.** Descriptive system evidence is legitimate. Stronger explanatory contributions must identify a falsifiable effect or relation involving mechanism, organization, state transition, information-conditioned action selection or boundaries. If the primary estimand cannot be recovered, abstain from forced Program routing.
- **S4 — Competence origins and alternatives.** Record who authored the SOP/decision tree, how action candidates were produced, which knowledge and human inputs were already available, system control/authority, cost, training overlap, decision-time information cutoff, evaluation independence, and competing explanations.
- **S5 — Evidence ceiling.** Scientific scope ≠ novelty ≠ claim validity. Restrict strong causal/transfer/RSI claims to supported levels; preserve source and failed claims. Null/negative effects remain valid results; target implementation labels cannot substitute for evidence.

## Proposed triage labels (not normative)

`OUTSIDE_SIS_CLAIM` | `SIS_SUPPORTING_ASSET` | `SIS_DESCRIPTIVE` | `SIS_EXPLANATORY_CANDIDATE` | `SIS_SCOPE_UNRESOLVED`.

These are review-only annotations. Do not overwrite authoritative R5.1 statuses `ROUTED`, `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`, `ROUTING_UNRESOLVED`, `SCIENTIFIC_NONCONFORMANT` and CL0–CL7 evidence levels.

## Boundary examples

- **Fixed SOP + verifier controlled experiment:** eligible SCF mechanism study even without a neural model. Matched information/resource and manipulation checks are needed for causal claims.
- **Fixed rule acting on new evidence to choose retry/stop/escalate:** eligible ASR policy question if *information-conditioned action selection* is the primary estimand; a mere if-statement is not yet identified policy value.
- **Deterministic symbolic planner on held-out task recombinations:** bounded capability evidence, not automatically an architecture/mechanism or broad-intelligence discovery.
- **Standalone sorter speed improvement:** routine algorithm engineering when no system-level task-intelligence target is articulated; do not broadly reject such algorithms as components of valid SIS systems.
- **Jev-like chooser with candidate option generator and domain SOP:** learned selection is one role among several; measure candidate-space quality, procedural priors, decisions, execution, authority and the independent evaluator separately.
- **Weak LLM + B3 SOP vs strong LLM + B1:** package performance is not a unique causal body effect if model, body, information and budget all vary. Include fair no-LLM comparisons.
- **Meta-optimizer revision:** may be within SIS as a science question; do not infer RSI from repeat attempts or changing evaluator, and do not force a program without a primary estimand.

## Outstanding theory objections

1. **Over-inclusion:** Without scientific question and target property restrictions, every program may appear to be SIS under a generic state/control representation.
2. **Under-inclusion:** Requiring learned cognition or causal proof at admission would reject fixed but generalizable systems and legitimate CL0/CL1 descriptive/measurement foundation studies.

**Conclusion:** Candidate scope rule is provisionally fit for SAR-01-C synthesis, not science-owner-adopted; SAR-01 remains OPEN. System-level explanatory scientific contribution and current evidential support must be decided separately.
