## C1 · Nonexclusive candidate modification loci

| Candidate | Requires | Does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| F0 | episode-local retry/replan/activation; no new durable change required | persistent self-learning |
| F1 | external skill/SOP/memory/procedural asset was **actually installed, invoked and persistent** | changed internal model, human-origin autonomy |
| F2 | **persistent effective decision function or learned model parameters** changed, regardless of physical storage | meta-improver update |
| F3_structural | the actual **future updater** `I_g→I_(g+1)` semantically changed, authorized, installed and invoked | positive prospective future-meta improvement, recursive depth or universal law |

F0–F3 are **change-locus labels, not an ordinal metric and not necessarily mutually exclusive**. F1+F2 can coexist if an SOP implements a real policy change; F1+F3 if a skill and its future updater are changed in one accepted package. Multi-target changes require separate target/version records. Proposed or uninvoked modifications cannot be called effective F3.

## C2 · Five orthogonal evidence/authority predicates

`ObservedGain` (can be confounded)  
`EffectiveVersionedTargetChange` (installed/invoked T change)  
`SystemOriginatedAction` (source actor ≠ approval authority)  
`EffectiveMetaOperatorChange` (modified installed/invoked I)  
`ProspectiveMetaImprovementEffect` (real independently evaluated future ability advantage)

They are **not nested intelligence levels**. Structural F3 may be human-authored; positive observed task improvement may arise with frozen I; F3 structural may produce zero or negative `theta_meta`. Record authors, proposers, approval, operator identity, authority, event times, permission, later rollbacks, outcome source separately.

## C3 · Strong meta-effect and recursion as separate claims

Review-only candidate:

`theta_meta = E_{j~D_future}[U(Apply(I_(g+1),T_g,j),E*) - U(Apply(I_g,T_g,j),E*)]`.

This estimates a **future operator-version effect** only under justified assignment/isolation, identical frozen starting T, matched legal information and opportunities, fixed evaluator, precommitted U including *actual* resources, failed proposals/no-change/rollback/risk, valid exposure cutoff and appropriate opportunity-level inference. Do not infer **self-origin** from version contrast. Do not infer **recursive multi-generation self-improvement** from a single version contrast: that claim needs actual I-updating-I lineage over future generations and separate pre-registered tests.

## C4 · Conditional identity/epistemic guards

- M1 known-pool activation vs M2 proposed `C_t` independently minted identity are conditional **audit representations**, never normatively adopted. Require creator/generation/version, permissions, effective graph, `do(intervention)` mapping and common estimand before causal comparison. Output equality never suffices.
- `E*` independently controlled from updater; hidden future Gold and scoring never accessible before decision. Changing E* or using evolve/promote tasks as future evaluation invalidates strong causal RSI attribution.
- Human-written program adopted by an agent is not automatically autonomous self-origin. Human approval of a system-proposed edit is separate from who created the edit.
