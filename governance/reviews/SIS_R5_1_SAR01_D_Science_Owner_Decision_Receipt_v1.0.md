## 1. Scope of conditional acceptance

The Science Owner accepted **Option A** in the source docket, **only as the scientific architecture review baseline** for subsequent SAR-02 through SAR-SYN. The following interpretation is accepted for *review use* (not production normative text):

1. **System-level conditional capability and scientific question over implementation labels.** A system may use a single cognitive component, LLM/LMM, Jev-like bounded learned decision model, fixed-rule controller, and/or advanced SOP. Model brand/type, generation mode, and component count do not automatically confer scientific status.
2. **Scientific scope, contribution and claim validity are distinct.** Descriptive, measurement and formal evidence may enter without causal identification; strong causal explanatory or transportability claims require appropriate evidence and explicit alternatives.
3. **Three claim objects stay distinct:** system capability; system intelligence as a high-level, multidimensional interpretive construct; causal contribution of any particular cognitive model or procedural body. No universal system-intelligence score or sixth core primitive.
4. **S1–S5 Scientific Scope Gate** remains an **audit procedure** rather than a new normative enum, Core ontology primitive or physical schema. Existing `ROUTED`, `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`, `ROUTING_UNRESOLVED`, `SCIENTIFIC_NONCONFORMANT` and CL0–CL7 remain authoritative in normative Master.
5. **Preservation by design intent (not executable proof):** five measured properties; Components/Relations/State/Observation/Control/Dynamics; SCF/SDR/ASR; CSCI/X2; claim-level estimand-first routing and independent scientific reference.

## 2. Two binding interpretation guards (still scientifically OPEN)

### C1 / O1 — Prevent over-inclusion

Routine automation, trivial scripts, a leaderboard score, or isolated algorithmic speedups do **not** automatically become core SIS explanatory discoveries merely because the setup can be represented using components, state and control. Require a defined conditional system property and a meaningful, defeasible scientific question/estimand. A deterministic system can qualify when it raises such a question. **Independent reviewer boundary-consistency testing remains unperformed.**

### C2 / O2 — Prevent under-inclusion

A study does **not** need completed causal identification to be admitted as legitimate descriptive/system measurement or formal evidence. Valid CL0/CL1 evidence, deterministic generalization and null/negative findings remain candidates for scientific accumulation, subject to claim ceilings. This does **not** promote them to mechanism identification or broad generalizable intelligence.

These conditions constrain how v0.8 is interpreted in SAR-02 and beyond; **they do not erase O1/O2 as outstanding theoretical objections**.
