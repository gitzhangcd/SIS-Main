# SAR-04-A｜F0–F3 & Meta-Improver Identity — Candidate Scientific Review Contract v0.1

> **REVIEW PROPOSAL ONLY · NOT PRESENT AS FROZEN R5.1 RSI SEMANTICS · SAR-04 OPEN.**  
> The terms F0/F1/F2/F3 are proposed *modification-locus/agency/persistence/evidence* classes, not numerical intelligence levels, a new Core ontology, an approved enum or SIS Primary Programs.

## C0 · Explicit separate identities and scientific boundaries

The auditor distinguishes:
- `T_g`: **improvee**, the versioned task system/harness/SOP/skill/model/policy subjected to improvement;
- `I_g`: **improver**, the effective proposal/selection/promotion procedure that produces subsequent `T` modifications;
- `E*`: external independent and frozen evaluation procedure, not modifiable by the acting system for the causal evaluation;
- `B`: declared composite-system / focal-component boundary; actor/owner/permissions, component versions, creator/source and data availability at each decision point;
- `D_{future}`: genuinely held-out later improvement tasks not exposed for the selection/promotion of the updated improver.

These are **candidate review measurement variables** and disclosures, **NOT newly adopted mandatory ScientificExperimentContract physical fields**.

## C1 · Four proposed loci of change

| Class | Minimum structural requirement | Must not claim from this alone |
|---|---|---|
| F0 | Transient episode retry/reflection/action/tool activation or temporary `R_t` change; no durable system change required | Persisted learning, durable autonomous self-modification, meta-improver change |
| F1 | Versioned SOP/memory/skill/procedural asset **actually persisted, approved and activated** | Model-internal learning or self-origin; an externally/human-authored asset can also be F1 at artifact level |
| F2 | Effective installed version change in learned component parameters **or the persistent decision/policy function itself** | New meta-improver or automatically improved ability to make further revisions. External storage of a policy table is not automatically F1. |
| F3 | A verifiable installed change `I_g→I_(g+1)` to the procedure for *making later system changes*, with identity and authority provenance | Positive **future meta-improver effect**, infinite recursion, global capability increase or independent efficacy without controlled prospective evaluation |

For mixed updates use **separate target/version records** rather than inventing a one-label hierarchy. If a new component with independent permission/identity is created under proposed M2, mark the **component boundary question unresolved** before forcing its update into F1/F2.

## C2 · Five evidence layers (separately testable)

**L0 observed outcome gain**: may result from extra calls, easier tasks, stronger model substitution, changed rubric or leakage.  
**L1 versioned change**: verify source ID, before/after hashes, actual promotion/activation and rollback receipt.  
**L2 autonomous update**: determine which system/human actor proposed and authorized the installed change; consent and permissions matter.  
**L3 meta-operator self-change**: establish that the **effective improvement algorithm**, not merely the target or its output, changed.  
**L4 prospective future-improvement advantage**: newly improved `I_(g+1)` must outperform `I_g` at **future update creation** under matched initial target, budget, information, protected evaluation, risk and costs.

**No L4/strong RSI evidence is produced by SAR-04-A.**

## C3 · Proposed controlled scientific comparison

From the **same frozen starting target `T_g`**, separately let `I_g` and `I_(g+1)` produce next-version target candidates. Freeze the scoring reference and provide matched calls, compute, time, task information, intervention eligibility, approval/rollback authority and hidden future task distribution. Define a *candidate* meta-improvement contrast:

`θ_meta = E[ U(Apply(I_(g+1),T_g), D_future, E*) - U(Apply(I_g,T_g), D_future, E*) ]`

The utility U should include task outcome, future-improvement efficiency, cost, risk, failed proposals and rollback. **It has not been estimated here.** The change from `T_g` to `T_(g+1)` by fixed `I_g` alone does not estimate `θ_meta`.

**Critical Kill Tests:** improver not actually modified; version proposed but not installed; only target/task improved; training/evolve set reused as future test; evaluator modified, model replaced with stronger pretrained component, unlimited extra retries/resources, non-comparable M1/M2 identity; human externally authored update misattributed as self-modification; omitted failed trials or reversals.
