## 0. North-star scientific question

Does SIS provide a scientifically coherent, discriminating, minimal, falsifiable, measurable and cumulative science of how system-level intelligence-related capabilities form, evolve and are regulated under explicit task, information, budget, boundary and risk constraints?

At this review stage:
- System Intelligence is selected as a **higher-level, multidimensional scientific construct** (not automatically a new primitive or single score).
- SIS is **cognitive-architecture-neutral** at the scientific level: generative LLMs/LMMs are a key initial empirical focus, not an ontological requirement. Specialized learned decision models (e.g. Jev-like), advanced non-LLM procedures, knowledge and feedback are eligible mechanisms/roles in appropriately framed system questions.
- Ordinary machine-learning model benchmarking and traditional automation are **not automatically independent core SIS discoveries**. They can supply comparison arms, baseline evidence or mechanism stress cases; the scientific question and claim strength determine status.
- The body may include advanced non-LLM SOPs, validators, rules, tools, persistent state and structured manuals. Do not smuggle in a stronger LLM as the body for core weak-brain compensation experiments.
- External system success does **not** prove the LLM necessary, and system capability does **not** prove generalized system intelligence. No-LLM controls and actual task-variation probes are mandatory for relevant claims.
- These are provisional science-owner review positions and candidates. **SAR-01 remains OPEN** until its explicit decision gate.

## 3. Immediate SAR-01 closure plan (do this before SAR-02)

**SAR-01-A — Four-system expressivity stress tests.** Four different scientific realizations:
1. Pure fixed-rule/SOP automation without learned cognitive decision core.
2. Specialized learned decision model (Jev-like) + B3 advanced SOP + evidence/manual, without a generative LLM.
3. Weak generative LLM + B1/B2/B3 body + versioned knowledge, plus strong-LLM minimal-body comparator.
4. Feedback-trained, or self-modifying, system with persistent revision and genuinely unseen task/skill recombinations.

For each, record explicitly: question/estimand, system boundary, source of procedural knowledge, cognitive-interface role, state/control, authorized information, risk/resource, comparison, expected falsifier, Program route, claim ceiling and status (primary scientific case / valid comparator / descriptive-only / outside scope). No invented empirical numbers.

**SAR-01-B — Boundary and counterexample gates.**
- No-LLM equivalence does not invalidate system capability; limits claims of necessary learned-cognitive contribution.
- A frozen SOP can generalize; an adaptive LLM may fail; task success ≠ generic intelligence.
- New task combinations/parameter perturbations and state/control disturbances distinguish precompiled answers from generalizable capability.
- LLM/Jev/architecture brand changes do not by themselves determine scientific classification.
- Reference/Gold leakage, future information, hidden stronger LLM, computation imbalance and SOP precompilation are explicit negative controls.

**SAR-01-C — Candidate identity and mother problem.** Return 1 compact definition, 3-5 scope rules, no more than 2 irreducible unresolved objections. Preserve existing 5 system properties, SCF/SDR/ASR, CSCI/X2 and 3 evidence roles unless separately justified.

**SAR-01-D — Interactive science-owner decision.** Present one decision card with recommendation, best skeptical objection and evidence gaps. User decides KEEP / MODIFY / RESTRUCTURE / DEFER. Mark the *scientific wording decision* as accepted for later synthesis while leaving empirical claims OPEN. Only then proceed to SAR-02.

## 4. Review-vs-validation gates

The review must never conflate:
- **Conceptual/semantic review (G-S):** internally coherent, minimally sufficient, nontrivial/falsifiable in principle; can be reviewed and conditionally accepted.
- **Operational conformance (G-O):** cross-referenceable contracts and executed positive/negative test fixtures; call UNEXECUTED where none were run.
- **Empirical scientific validation (G-E):** actual study arms, pre-registration, independent benchmarks, uncertainty and boundary tests. No hypothesis becomes established through discussion, an AI review, or a YAML schema alone.

A master may freeze scientific conventions while leaving untested research hypotheses OPEN. Do not block an entire specification merely because new causal laws have not yet been established, but do block unsupported assertions that they **have** been proved.

## 5. Interaction and progress protocol

- **One scientific issue at a time.** Assistant first supplies concise current-text basis, first-principles interpretation, strongest alternative/skeptic attack, minimum change, and 2-4 concrete failure tests. Science owner gives preliminary direction; no automatic approval from a choice.
- **One decision card to close each topic**, with explicit unresolved questions, status and downstream impact. Never silently replace a source definition.
- **Cross-topic dependencies:** SAR-01 → SAR-02 → SAR-03 → SAR-04 → SAR-05 → SAR-06 → SAR-07 → SAR-08 → SAR-09 → SAR-10 → SAR-SYN. Work on evidence registers and counterexample preparation can run in parallel but no topic is declared approved out of sequence.
- **Progress ledger updates after each topic:** statuses `NOT_STARTED | IN_REVIEW | DECISION_PENDING | REVIEW_ACCEPTED | REOPEN | BLOCKED`; preserve what remains OPEN in evidence and release gates. Report overall progress with each completed subtask, not only the next step.
- **Source discipline:** cite R5.1 candidate clause/part for source-derived statements and distinguish independent research hypotheses. Current external papers are suggestive, not substitutes for actual SIS experiments.
- **Version/authority discipline:** review only on draft GitHub branch; do not merge into `main` or label normative R5.1 FROZEN without science-owner approval and independent release governance.
