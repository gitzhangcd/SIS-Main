## Six minimal review clauses

**S0 · Declared scientific boundary and time.** `Ω=(Task,Environment,Information,Budget,Constraints,Risk)`. Identify focal component F (exact frozen weights/adapters/config and permitted minimal baseline B0), whole system S, relevant components and relations, evaluation/decision time. `Localization L(r|F,S,t)` is relative to component and system boundary, not a magnitude or causal estimator. Source: R5.1 §5 and §39A–C **proposed**.

**S1 · Separate component ability from system achievement.** ModelID/brand and isolated benchmark are not a `ComponentCapabilityConstruct`. Define task/risk-relevant measured multidimensional ability c with proxies, measurement quality, uncertainty and test boundaries. Retrieved documents/prompts do not become internal learned parameters; external strong specialist tooling can contribute independently. Source: §36 core; Part VII-A §39D/F/G/R **proposed**.

**S2 · Decompose supports by causal role.** Distinguish external information I, scaffold/organization/verification mechanism M, specialist non-LLM or LLM component capabilities Cext, memory/state, runtime human H, independent expert reference E*, author/approver roles and actual budget. Human evaluator cannot be silently counted as runtime system intelligence. Source: §11–13/§24/§84; Part VII-A §39H/J/N/O/P/Q/V **proposed**.

**S3 · Conditional mechanism and interaction contrast.** Propose causal `Δ_M(c,i;Ω,R)=E[Y|do(M=1),c,i,Ω,R]-E[Y|do(M=0),c,i,Ω,R]` and `Ψ=Δ_M(c_weak)-Δ_M(c_strong)` conditional on information, task, risk and resources. 2×2 (c weak/strong × support OFF/ON), with minimum 2×2×2 split between `M` and external `I` when both may change. Positive `Ψ` could support **bounded substitution/moderation under controlled evidence**; not universal equivalence of weak/strong model internal cognition. Test complementarity/saturation/threshold/null/harm as rivals. CSCI is cross-cutting, not fourth Primary Program.

**S4 · New review-only Cognitive Floor.** Test a task/mechanism/metric/threshold-specific **lower envelope** `A(M,Ω,τ,q)={c:Pr(Y≥τ|do(M),c,Ω)≥q}`; floor is `min_partial_order(A)` when identified. It need not be a single scalar, monotonic, portable across domains or even identifiable. Mechanism use-eligibility threshold differs from component's general cognition. A no-LLM/no-model negative control must first pass task eligibility and positivity; do not force a zero-model control on an ineligible scientific interpretation task. No “Cognitive Floor” is source-frozen in R5.1.

**S5 · Correct scientific claim language and no schema inflation.** One `ScientificClaim→PrimaryEstimand→Conformance→ProgramRouting`. SCF for controlled mechanism effect, SDR for recovery/trajectory, ASR for conditional choose/retrieve/verify/escalate. CSCI/X2 cross-cutting. Exactly `ROUTED, DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY, ROUTING_UNRESOLVED, SCIENTIFIC_NONCONFORMANT` canonical statuses. Outcome/equivalence without valid causal identification is descriptive, not mechanism proof. Reuse single §84 `ScientificExperimentContract`, no new mandatory core field, no owner-adopted Part VII-A, M2 `C_t`, F0–F3 norm or Master edit.
