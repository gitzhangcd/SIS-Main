## 2. Precisely accepted — conditional scientific audit interpretation only

**C1 · Claim and Estimand before Program.** `ScientificClaim→PrimaryEstimand→ScientificConformance→ProgramRouting`; never route by paper, system, technology, model/framework label or the mere word “feedback”. One identified ScientificClaim has one Primary Program, while an experiment may produce separately justified claims for different Programs.

**C2 · Retain exactly SCF / SDR / ASR.**
- `SCF`: target effect of system mechanism, organization or component interaction on a system-level property. A strong **causal** claim must have valid comparison/manipulation/resource-information evidence; an instrument/tool merely existing does not establish mechanism effect.
- `SDR`: target time-/transition-/trajectory-/propagation-/containment-/recovery-dependent functional; a timestamp or endpoint result alone does not determine the Program.
- `ASR`: target legally information-conditioned **endogenous action-selection/policy value**, including possible `NO_CHANGE`, `ABSTAIN` or stop. Policy choice, permitted execution and dynamics are different scientific roles. Policy value may be evaluated on long trajectories; time-dependence alone does not convert it to SDR.

**C3 · Conditional precedence, not forced reclassification.** `DecisionSelection > Trajectory > StaticMechanismEffect` helps resolve a *recoverable primary estimand*; it is not a keyword fallback and does not repair an unspecified claim. An unsplittable, genuinely irreducible coupled estimand remains a **potential falsifier under RA1**, not an invented fourth Primary Program by default.

**C4 · Preserve exactly four canonical routing statuses:** `ROUTED`, `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`, `ROUTING_UNRESOLVED`, `SCIENTIFIC_NONCONFORMANT`. Scope vs contribution vs claim validity differ: valid CL0/CL1 descriptive/formal/fixed-SOP findings stay representable, while invalid stronger causal claims can be downgraded or rejected **at claim level**. A null/negative controlled mechanism effect can remain a scientifically valid target.

**C5 · Overlays stay cross-cutting.** `CSCI/X1` is component **capability construct × mechanism** effect modification, not mere ModelID grouping; `X2` distinguishes replication, performance transfer, causal transportability and mechanism invariance. Neither becomes a new Primary Program or universal scalar.

**C6 · SAR-02-D conditional M1/M2 review only.** `M1` a known/bounded potential `C^*` with effective activation; `M2` a proposed dynamic `C_t` identity representation **not normatively adopted**. When attempting the **same scientific estimand**, compare component boundary/identity, creator/generation/version/events, effective graph, actual action/authorization, legal information cutoff, task/resource/budget/risk/horizon, intervention correspondence and target functional. Equal output does **not** prove causal/identity equivalence. `REGIME_COMPARABILITY_UNRESOLVED` is an **audit annotation**, not a fifth canonical routing status.

**C7 · Full falsification rights remain.** Independent-researcher failures of reliable routing or a genuine joint `Q^*` not expressible in three Programs warrant revisiting the architecture under R5.1 RA1/RA2 and other RA gates, **not** protecting the accepted review baseline as a frozen theory.

## 3. Skeptical argument preserved — why conditional ≠ proven

The strongest counterargument is **definition-induced classification**: if authors write a desired PrimaryEstimand in the language of one Program, same-author fixtures will reproduce that label, but independent researchers may disagree on the real claim target. The choice to regard a policy affecting long-term recovery as ASR (policy-value estimand), SDR (trajectory estimand) or SCF (mechanism effect) is valid **only if the actual ScientificClaim and evaluation contract specify those distinct quantities**. To claim independent reliability, two blind external coders and adjudicated Gold are still required. The 58/58 local source/fixture checks are **not independent coding agreement**.

A second falsifier is a **joint strategy–structure–trajectory–risk** estimand that cannot be losslessly assigned to one Program. A third is M1/M2 changing the component causal intervention identity while reporting the same output. Both remain **OPEN** scientific challenges, not resolved by the user’s Option A choice.

Theoretical **formal irreducibility** has not been proved; an independent experiment could help test empirical discriminant validity/utility but cannot by itself establish universal mathematical impossibility of reducing one Program to another.
