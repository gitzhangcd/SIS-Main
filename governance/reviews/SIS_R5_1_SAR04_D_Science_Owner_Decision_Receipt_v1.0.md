## 2. Precisely conditionally accepted — candidate scientific audit interpretation only

**A1 — Orthogonal RSI change loci, not a hierarchy.** `RSI-F0` transient episode behavior; `RSI-F1` effectively installed/invoked/persistent external SOP/skill/memory/procedural asset; `RSI-F2` effective persistent policy/learned-model function change independent of physical storage; `RSI-F3_structural` actual future *improver* `I_g → I_(g+1)` functionally changed and lawfully installed/invoked. These labels are **nonordinal and potentially overlapping** (e.g. F1+F2/F1+F3) across a multi-asset experiment; never substitute a single highest “intelligence” grade.

**A2 — System target, future improver and evaluator identities separate.** `T_g` is frozen initial improvee, `I_g` the authorized effective proposal/selection/promotion process, and `E*` the independently owned external assessment reference. Version hash/semantic diff, provenance (author/proposer/approver), actual installation and invocation, permissions, event time and rollback must be separately attributable. Names, stored files or reported outputs are insufficient evidence.

**A3 — Structural F3, autonomy, strong prospective effectiveness and recursive depth are independent scientific claims.**
- Human-authored installed improver **can be structural F3** but not automatically self-originated.
- Improving `T_g` with unchanged `I_g` does **not** prove F3.
- Structural F3 does **not** establish that the future improver version is more effective.
- Even a positive single future operator-version effect would **not** prove multi-generation recursive plasticity, unlimited growth or a universal intelligence law.

**A4 — Strong F3 evidence remains unexecuted.** A scientifically strong claim calls for matched old/new `I` versions on identical frozen `T_g`, independent untouched future improvement opportunities, protected fixed `E*`, legal information cutoff, matching intervention opportunity/tool/compute budget, recorded **actual** resources, task risks and human participation, and all attempted changes including failures, NO_CHANGE and rollback. Analyze **future improvement opportunity** as the unit, with appropriate randomization/paired isolation and uncertainty. **No real version contrast or real causal effect has yet been tested/estimated.**
