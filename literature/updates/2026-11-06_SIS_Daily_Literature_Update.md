## Five scientific implications
1. **Authority requires evidence and decision jurisdiction:** reviewer suggestions need not be binding. The correct-proposal corruption/repair tradeoff is conditional on model and gate.
2. **Operational telemetry is not self-authorizing:** give models verified tool-health snapshots while host retains dispatch and enforcement. Evaluate net regret under fault injection.
3. **A passed verifier is not proof of correct task semantics:** distinguish verifier acceptance, formal specification binding, and semantic requirement coverage; also version-control clinical categories.
4. **Causal replay must reproduce the actual state:** a matching snapshot/hash may omit random-stream position; measure replay fidelity before estimating a history intervention.
5. **Science capability and physical actionability require independent Gold:** FrontierPhysics and BEAKER diagnose different constructs; neither offline expert rubric nor abstract QA proves a real-world autonomous lab result.
