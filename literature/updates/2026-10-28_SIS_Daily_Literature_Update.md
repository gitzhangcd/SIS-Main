## Literature selection and cross-cutting synthesis

1. **Workload-and-shift effects:** SkillShift + AOS extend SDR from fixed tasks to nonstationary skills and concurrent task-arrival/resource regimes. Distinct from SWE-Shift/Async-RBench; maintain distinct study identities and benchmark lineage.
2. **Viability ≠ goal completion:** SABench explicitly measures unauthorized side effects even after task success; encode complete action-state effect and scope of legitimate authorization, not only terminal outcome.
3. **Representation and evaluator validity:** Scoped Semantic Invariance and expert-calibrated judgment support MEAS/ID/EG, but bounded KG semantics and field-specific human-Gold impose construct limits.
4. **Error feedback and risk propagation:** preregistered Lessons from Luck gives conditionally stronger causal evidence of stochastic-outcome contamination, while IE-Prop gives prediction gains only; never turn AUROC into a causal propagation claim.
5. **Policy utility vs signals:** calibrated partner reliance and task-adaptive rubrics are ASR candidates; whether they improve action net value under independent Gold, budget controls and distribution shift remains open.

### Benchmark and contract candidate deltas
- `SHIFT×SKILL×EXECUTOR`: Context/Perception/Execution Shift with matched replay, adaptation cost and capability retention.
- `CONCURRENT_WORKLOAD×INTAKE_POLICY×BUDGET`: matched isolated/stream scores, task claim rate, CPU/memory, nonlinearity and stochastic failure; compare fixed concurrency=2.
- `GOAL_SUCCESS×AUTHORIZED_SIDE_EFFECTS`: independent transaction/effect ledger, ambiguity/overreach, wrong object, goal-vs-viability.
- `SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE×KG_GROUNDING`: bounded ontology, human-checked equivalence, selective/broad grounding, right-to-wrong regressions.
- `NOISY_OUTCOME×LESSON_POLICY`: registered bad-luck/good-luck tool contrast, irrelevant-memory, explicit uncertainty vs persistence.
- `PARTNER_EVIDENCE×EXPECTED_POLICY_UTILITY`: analytical oracle for verify/rely/assist, zero-VOI probes and cost.
- `PROCESS_RUBRIC×EXTERNAL_GOLD`: frozen data science evaluator, full attempts/search compute, no same-loop judge authority.
- `EXPERT_GOLD×EVALUATOR_VERSION`: calibrate human interrater consistency before judge agreement; domain transfer separated.
